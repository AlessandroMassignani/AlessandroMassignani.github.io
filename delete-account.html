Delete your NineShift account

You can delete your NineShift account directly in the app from the account/profile controls. If you cannot access the app, use this form to request deletion.

What deletion does

  • Your NineShift authentication account and account-linked profile data are deleted.
  • Active matches are closed and your account is detached from past match records where needed to preserve game integrity.
  • Some limited transaction, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records may be retained where necessary or required by law.

Requests are processed after we verify that the request relates to the account identified by the email address provided.
